Does The Core send emails on its own?

No. It leaves replies in your drafts and you send them yourself.

Can The Core spend money or pay bills?

No. It prepares the numbers. Any payment is a step you take.

Where does my business data live with The Core?

On your own computer or a server you choose. The text it works on goes to Claude, run by Anthropic.

How long does it take to install The Core?

Less than a working day. Installs so far have taken 3 to 8 hours, depending on your tools.

What do I need to run The Core?

Your own Claude subscription and a computer that stays on. If yours doesn't, I set up a small server.
