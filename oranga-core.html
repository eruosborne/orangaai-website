Questions

Frequently asked questions.

What does it cost?

It's a one-off install, not a subscription. We'll give you the exact figure on the Admin Audit, once we know what your business needs. And the install is covered by a 7-day money-back guarantee.

How long does it take to set up?

The install takes less than a working day, about 3 to 8 hours depending on your tools. Then we stay with you for 30 days while it beds in, with a call about once a week.

Will it send anything without asking me?

No. It prepares the reply, the follow-up or the invoice reminder, and you press send. It can't contact a customer, move money or commit you to anything on its own.

I've tried AI before. It didn't stick.

Most owners we talk to have. Oranga Core is set up around your business before it does anything: what you do, who you serve and how you write. It's installed for you and walked through with you, so you don't have to learn how to make it work.

I'm not very technical. Will I manage?

Yes. You talk to it in plain English, like a new hire in their first week. If it isn't sure what you mean, it asks.

What if it doesn't work for me?

If it hasn't given you real time back or a noticeable productivity boost within 7 days of handover, you get a full refund.

Which tools does it work with?

It connects to your mail, calendar and accounts, so it works on your real business, not a demo. Other tools depend on your setup, so we confirm what's possible on the Admin Audit.
