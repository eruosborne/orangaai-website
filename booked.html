The questions people ask before the call, answered.

Anything else, ask on the call.

Does The Core send emails on its own?

No. It prepares replies and leaves them in your drafts. You read them, change what you like and send them yourself. That’s how the system is built, not a setting.

Where does my business data live with The Core?

On your own computer that stays on, or on a server set up for you that you pay the host for. You choose the host and the country it’s in.

Does the AI behind The Core train on my data?

The text The Core works on is sent to Claude, run by Anthropic. Whether Anthropic may use your chats to improve its models depends on your Claude plan and a setting in your own account. On a Pro plan the choice is yours.

Who can see my files once The Core is installed?

During the install I can see what you see, including documents. I don’t read them. Your files stay with you and Oranga AI keeps no copy.

Am I locked in to The Core?

No. Your business data stays yours. So does everything built on top of it. Your knowledge base stays usable if the agreement ends. Once it’s paid in full the licence is permanent.

What if The Core gets something wrong in front of a customer?

It can’t reach a customer on its own. Every reply waits in your drafts for you to read first, so a mistake stops with you.

Got questions before the call?

You’ll get a couple of emails from me before we talk. No bots, no spam. Reply to any of them and it comes straight to me.

About the follow-up

Expect one or two short emails a day until the call: one now, one the night before and one an hour out. Each one helps you show up ready.

Why we do it this way

Busy owners decide quickly. Giving you the answers up front means that by the time we talk, you’re a clear yes, a clear no, or ready to decide.
