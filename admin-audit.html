Common questions

A few things people wonder before booking.

"Is this just a sales call?"

No. The call is a diagnosis first. We look at your business, identify where Oranga Core would and wouldn't help, and give you a straight answer. If it's not the right fit right now, we'll tell you.

"I'm not very technical, will I follow along?"

Completely. We don't talk in technical terms. We talk about your business: which tasks, what changes, what it costs. No jargon.

"How long does it take to get set up?"

The install itself takes less than a working day, about 3 to 8 hours depending on your tools. Then we stay with you for 30 days while it beds in.

"Will it send anything without asking me?"

No. It prepares the reply, the follow-up or the invoice reminder, and you press send. It can't contact a customer, move money or commit you to anything on its own. See the six things it never does alone.

"I've tried AI before. It didn't stick."

Most owners we talk to have. Oranga Core is set up around your business before it does anything: what you do, who you serve and how you write. It's installed for you and walked through with you, so you don't have to learn how to make it work.

"What does it cost?"

It's a one-off install, not a subscription. We'll give you the exact figure on the call, once we know what your business needs. And the install is covered by a 7-day money-back guarantee.
