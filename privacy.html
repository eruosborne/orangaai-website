Privacy policy

Oranga AI is a sole-operator business run by Eru Osborne, Gold Coast, Australia. This page covers what's collected through forms on orangaai.com and how it's used.

What's collected. When you fill in a form on this site (a giveaway entry, a strategy call booking, a newsletter signup), we collect what you type into it — typically your name, business details, and email address.

How it's used. To respond to you, deliver what you asked for (a booked call, a giveaway entry, a setup guide), and to send occasional updates or offers about Oranga Core. You can unsubscribe from those at any time by replying to any email or contacting us directly.

Who sees it. Only Eru Osborne. Data isn't sold. Form details aren't shared with anyone outside Oranga AI.

Advertising and analytics. This site uses the Meta (Facebook and Instagram) Pixel and Google Analytics. They record which pages you visit and whether you complete an action, such as finishing the quiz or booking a call. Oranga AI uses that to measure results and to show its ads to people who have visited the site. Your name and email address from forms are not sent to Meta. You can opt out of personalised ads in your Facebook and Instagram ad settings.

Where it's stored. In our own email and business tools, kept only as long as it's useful to the relationship (an active lead, a customer, or someone who's asked to stay on the list).

Questions or removal requests. Email support@orangaai.com and we'll sort it directly.
