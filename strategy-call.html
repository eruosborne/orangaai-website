No. The call is a diagnosis first. We look at your business, identify where Oranga Core would and wouldn't help, and give you a straight answer. If it's not the right fit right now, we'll tell you.

"I'm not very technical, will I follow along?"

Completely. We don't talk in technical terms. We talk about your business: which tasks, what changes, what it costs. No jargon.

The install itself takes less than a working day, about 3 to 8 hours depending on your tools. Then we stay with you for 2 weeks while it beds in.

Most owners we talk to have. Oranga Core is set up around your business before it does anything: what you do, who you serve and how you write. It's installed for you and walked through with you, so you don't have to learn how to make it work.
